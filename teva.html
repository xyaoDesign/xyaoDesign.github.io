Project Overview

At Teva Pharmaceuticals, I work in the Engineering Department as part of the company's modernization and automation efforts on the manufacturing floor.

My role brings design into a GMP-regulated production environment across three areas: leading an AR project that guides operators through machinery assembly, supporting the introduction of robotics on the manufacturing floor, and creating graphic design and interior visualizations that help improve the workplace.

The Design Challenge

On a manufacturing floor, new technology only helps if the people using it can understand it, trust it, and fit it into the way they already work.

In pharmaceutical production, procedures must be followed precisely and changes must hold up to quality review. The challenge is to introduce AR guidance and automation in a way that reduces complexity for operators rather than adding to it, while meeting the requirements of a GMP-regulated environment.

My Role & Design Approach

As Application Developer and Site Project Lead for the AR project, I connect design, technology, and operations, turning complex procedures into experiences operators can follow with confidence.

AR Project for Machinery Operation

SOP-Aligned AR Workflow

Designed an SOP-aligned AR assembly workflow for pharmaceutical production machinery on a vendor-provided platform, translating a 70+ step assembly SOP into clear and visual step-by-step guidance shaped around operators' terminology and the GMP-regulated production environment.

Cross-Functional Implementation

Drove the project from initial exploration toward formal site implementation, coordinating Production, QA, IT, and global teams to align the workflow with operational requirements and existing machinery.

Visual SOP Instructions

Enhanced SOPs with annotated visual instructions to improve procedural clarity and workflow consistency for production operators.

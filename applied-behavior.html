What is This Project

This project explores how augmented reality can be used to support Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) therapy for children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). It focuses on creating a practical, clinician-guided tool that enhances learning while reducing some of the time, cost, and material demands of traditional ABA sessions.

The core of the project is an AR application that uses physical cards with QR codes. When scanned on a tablet, each card displays interactive 3D stimuli designed to teach foundational concepts, starting with color identification. The experience mirrors how therapists typically present learning trials, while adding structured feedback, automatic data collection, and consistent reinforcement for correct and incorrect responses.

In addition to AR-based instruction, the project integrates eye-tracking technology to study how children visually engage with learning stimuli. By understanding what features attract attention such as color, shape, movement, or brightness the system can inform the design of more effective and engaging instructional materials in the future.

Overall, this project combines behavioral science, immersive technology, and data-driven design to investigate whether augmented reality can make ABA therapy more efficient, accessible, and engaging, while maintaining the clinical rigor required in real-world therapeutic settings.

What I Did

As the developer and technical artist, I helped strengthen product reliability and speed up delivery by pairing structured problem-solving with hands-on execution across the stack. I resolved a long-standing asset rendering defect that had blocked production for three months, closing it within my first two weeks by combining systematic debugging with targeted experiments. I also simplified user preparation materials from six pages to three, cutting onboarding effort by 50%, and introduced an asset-library capability that lets users customize and organize assets. This reduced confusion, improved task retention, and streamlined day-to-day workflows. Additionally, I independently delivered end-to-end features across both front-end and back-end, removing dependencies and accelerating the roadmap by roughly two months while improving overall team throughput.

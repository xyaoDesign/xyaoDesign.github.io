Background Story

In 1856, time travelers from the future entered a new timeline, bringing advanced technology that transformed the lives of the natives. But years later, war erupted over resources, and poison spread through the soil, forcing survivors to seek refuge beneath the ocean floor. The time travelers helped build homes under the sea but eventually left, knowing the world was beyond saving. By 1976 in that timeline, sixty-two years after they had vanished, even the ocean was contaminated. Humanity disappeared—only the rusting structures remained. And now, from a world where all else has withered, a single mutated flower has finally survived and bloomed. No longer soft or green, but metallic and sharp, it stands alone in the snow. Different, altered, but still alive. A quiet trace of hope within the remnants.

Scene Concept

The scene begins with a frozen landscape and a single flower, still closed, beneath a hovering temple. As time passes, the flower begins to bloom, not in a natural way, but with mechanical movement, matching its metallic texture. A red, lightly emissive pistil appears at the center of the flower, suggesting it is no longer a normal plant, but likely a product of radiation and contamination. In the background, the sun moves across the sky from left to right, up to down. Near the end of the scene, it passes through an open space in the structure, revealing itself for the first time. But instead of a warm, bright sun, it is completely black, further emphasizing that this world is broken, unnatural, and far from what it once was.
